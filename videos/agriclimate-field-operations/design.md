# Field-operations film — design truth

Concept: every camera move follows one piece of field evidence from the monitoring map to a human decision; typography annotates the working product rather than replacing it.

## Canvas and palette
1920×1080, 30 fps, 246 seconds. Safe inset 64px. Main product act uses the app's off-white `oklch(0.985 0.005 130)` and deep emerald `oklch(0.45 0.12 148)`; the alert hook and engineering act use an emerald-tinted ink #101f1b with off-white text. Danger red derives from `oklch(0.55 0.19 28)`; readable pale red #ffb5a8 on dark. Small muted text #52645a on light / #b3c7ba on dark.

## Type
Locally embedded Geist regular/medium/semibold/bold, Geist Mono regular/medium, and Noto Sans Bengali regular/bold for Bengali only. Display 60–100px, hero numerals 140–180px, annotations 28–36px, evidence footer 22px minimum. Source code 27–32px. Product footage preserves its own typography; focused crops and separate large callouts carry the reading burden.

## Frame grammar
Top rail: film name, act, scene number. Second rail: concise outcome headline. Center: real product footage or a source-grounded diagram/code panel. Bottom rail: exact source/provenance, capture/static distinction, continuous chapter progress. Product footage gets an intentional 2px frame, not invented device chrome. Mobile captures are tall, left/right offset; supporting copy leads to their semantic action.

## Depth and motion
Focal element = actual interaction or source code. Edge anchors = rail and evidence footer. Supporting detail = one to three larger readouts, never speculative values. Background = flat branded canvas with a thin coordinate/grid motif only in the map/engineering acts.

Compose viewport-change, discrete-text-sequence, stat-bars-and-fills. Small panel entrances settle in 0.4–0.6s, data stays still long enough to read. Hard cuts between farmer and officer. Directional-wipe catalog primitive adapted to emerald solid cover at act boundaries; no RGB/glitch effects. Avoid applying color filters to real app footage.

## Audio
Warm local narration with ample reading holds. Original deterministic 80 BPM additive-keys instrumental because no authenticated music catalog is available. Bundled soft ticks and notification chime. Bed receives dynamic spectral carve against the narration group. Space immediately after notification; no trailer effects.

## Honesty rules
Never relabel seed alerts as current warnings. Never imply channel metadata proves telecom delivery. Crop scores and telemetry must match captures. Test count comes from this run. Live NASA failures are shown as failures, not substituted successful output. Commands in the outro are setup instructions, not claimed execution of clone/install.
